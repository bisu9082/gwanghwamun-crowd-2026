# BTS Gwanghwamun (21 Mar 2026): what officials actually said about crowd size

Source file: `/tmp/official/official_statements.json` has 26 items, and I opened every URL in it through Exa. Four items are primary government pages: news.seoul.go.kr, two korea.kr pages, and the Pressna verbatim MCST release is cited in the notes. Everything else is a press report quoting officials. No official transcript, minutes or text-message archive entry was opened.

## 1. Conditional/capacity wording versus forecast wording

**The original police statement was conditional (9 Feb, regular briefing by SMPA chief Park Jeong-bo).**
- Kyunghyang's verbatim version: "(세종대로) 전 차로에 인파가 꽉 들어서면 23만명 정도 … 숭례문까지 인파가 더 모이면 26만명이 될 것으로 생각된다". In English: "if every lane of Sejong-daero is packed, about 230,000 … if the crowd gathers further to Sungnyemun, 260,000."
- Yonhap also has him hedging that day: "실제 그렇게 될지 모르겠으나 최대한 많은 인원이 모일 것으로 예상해 대비하고 있다" ("I don't know whether it will actually turn out that way, but we are preparing on the assumption that the maximum number will gather").
- Basis: the "㎡당 2명" (2 persons/m²) density basis is first attributed to him on the same day, 9 Feb (Dong-A and Sports Donga). This is a reporter paraphrase, not a direct quote.
- The same briefing also gave the ticketed base as about 35,000 (Kyunghyang).

**Where conditionality was dropped**

| Date | Who dropped it | Wording | Where it appeared |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 Feb | Headlines and leads the same day | "26만명 몰릴 듯", "26만명 운집 예상" | Yonhap, Dong-A, YTN, The Fact, Seoul Shinmun |
| 15 Mar | Police themselves (SMPA plan announcement) | "경찰은 관람객 최대 26만명이 모일 것으로 예상하고" | Near-identical text in Newsis, Seoul Economic Daily and Edaily, so it evidently follows the police release |
| 16 Mar | Seoul city itself (primary) | "전 세계 약 26만 명의 관람객이 방문할 것으로 예상되는 대규모 공연입니다" | news.seoul.go.kr notice from the Gwanghwamun Square Project Division. Plain forecast, no condition |
| 16 Mar | Government portal (primary) | "최대 26만 명의 인파가 몰릴 것으로 예상된다" | korea.kr K-공감, summarising the MOIS 11 Mar measures |
| 20 Mar | MCST (primary) | "관람객 약 17만 명에서 최대 26만 명이 모일 것으로 예상되는 만큼" | korea.kr joint release and the Pressna verbatim release |

- On 15 Mar the 2/m² figure turns into a gate-closure threshold rather than the basis of the forecast.
- The 20 Mar MCST range was the stated context for the first-ever 공연장 재난 위기경보 '주의' (performance-venue disaster alert, "caution" level).

**The MOIS 11 Mar release itself appears to contain no 26만.**
- The release-derived texts in Aju, Yonhap and Nocut say only "관람객 2만2천여명을 포함해 대규모 인파" ("a large crowd including about 22,000 ticketed spectators").
- Newsis added "경찰 추산 최대 26만명" (up to 260,000 by police estimate), and Dong-A/News1 added "최대 26만 명".
- Uncertainty: I did not open the original MOIS release page.

**Other officials used lower or different numbers**
- 6 Mar, Seoul Council member Lee Jong-bae: "약 17만 명".
- 9 Mar, Mayor Oh Se-hoon: "17만 인파 앞에서는".
- 18 Mar, police briefing (Lee Byung-chul, Newsis paraphrase): real admissible capacity was about 100,000, with about 22,000 in the core zone.
- So by 18 Mar the police were already describing a capacity ceiling well below 26만.

## 2. Is the 23 March worst-case re-description confirmed?

**Yes.** It was said at the SMPA regular briefing on 23 Mar and appears in at least eight outlets I opened:
- Maeil Business, two desks. The entertainment-desk version is the fullest: "저희가 26만명을 말한 건 숭례문까지 인파가 차면 26만명까지 들어올 수 있다는 것 … 경찰은 최악의 상황을 대비한다"
- Yonhap: "숭례문까지 차면 26만명이 들어올 수 있다는 최악의 상황을 대비했다"
- MBC, Seoul Economic Daily, Kyunghyang, Seoul Shinmun, SPOTV, and Channel A (paraphrase).
- Companion line in all of them: "시민 안전과 관련해서는 과도하게 대응해야 한다" ("on citizens' safety, one should over-respond").

**Caveat on the exact verb.** It differs by outlet:
- MBC has "최대 26만 명이 몰릴 것이라고 이야기한 것은 최악의 상황을 대비한 것" (a forecast verb).
- Maeil Business and Yonhap have "들어올 수 있다" (a capacity verb).

The substance is consistent: a conditional, worst-case figure tied to crowds reaching Sungnyemun. This matches the 9 Feb conditional wording, so it is a re-emphasis of the original framing rather than a new invention. However, the police's own 15 Mar announcement had presented it unconditionally.

## 3. Seoul city statements

**"20만~30만" (200,000–300,000)**
- I found no primary or direct quote.
- The phrase "서울시는 20만∼30만명가량이 … 찾을 것으로 예상했다" originates in Yonhap's 22 Mar article (AKR20260322034100530). It is unattributed and was then copied by YTN, Munhwa, Digital Times, Herald and Mediawatch.
- Earlier press estimates ("20만 명 이상" in Hankook, 4 Feb; "20만~30만명을 넘길 가능성", MT, 9 Feb) are journalists' or industry estimates, not city quotes.
- The user-supplied "최소 20만~30만" from the mayor on 19 Mar was **not found**. His 19 Mar Facebook post said "수십만 명이 한꺼번에 움직이는 도심" ("a city centre where hundreds of thousands move at once") and "마음껏, 안전하게 즐겨 달라" ("enjoy it to the fullest, safely").

**Post-event**
- 22 Mar city release (via Yonhap/MBC/Kyunghyang): the city cited the organiser's figure of 104,000.
- 26–27 Mar: the living-population figure of 75,927 comes from Dong-A's and Yonhap's analysis of city open data, plus city-official comments. No city press release on it was found.

## 4. Disaster and safety text messages

- Seoul city sent a safety guidance message (안전안내문자) on the morning of 20 Mar, in Korean and then English, geofenced to the Gwanghwamun area.
- It gave road-closure times. The full Korean text is in Sidae, and Dong-A corroborates it.
- **No crowd figure appears in the message text.**
- The exact send time and the safekorea.go.kr archive record were not verified.
- The 21 Mar messages are referenced (MK: "재난문자 폭주", i.e. a flood of alerts), but I did not find their full texts.

## 5. National Assembly and Seoul Council

- **National Assembly:** I found no questioning or minutes about 26만 or over-mobilisation, March–April 2026. Result: not found, which is not proof of absence.
- **Seoul Council:** only the pre-event 6 Mar remark (17만). No post-event council questioning was found.

## 6. Union statements

- **20 Mar, KGEU Seoul HQ (전공노 서울지역본부) statement:** "안전대책의 필요성이 차출의 정당성으로 이어지지 않는다 … 행정력 남용" ("the need for safety measures does not justify conscripting staff … abuse of administrative power"). Sources: Herald, Kyunghyang, NGO News, Wolyo.
- **22 Mar, head Jeon Eun-sook to Yonhap:** "공공기관이 아닌 사기업에서 하는 공연까지 공무원을 동원하는 게 문제" ("the problem is mobilising public servants even for a private company's concert").
- **25 Mar, second statement:** officials were used as "가성비 좋은 안전요원" ("cost-effective safety staff"). Source: Tenbizt / MK 뉴스에이드.
- None of these union texts cite 26만 directly.
- The union's own website text was not opened.

## 7. The basis (2 persons/m²) and when it was first said

- First found on 9 Feb, attributed to chief Park (paraphrase in Dong-A and Sports Donga).
- The area is described as Gwanghwamun to Sungnyemun. One MT paraphrase (24 Mar) instead says "숭례문부터 안국역" (Sungnyemun to Anguk station), which is inconsistent with the other accounts.
- No official document giving the area in m² was found.

## 8. Deterrence

**No official statement using 26만 to deter attendance was found.**
- The closest is the 17 Mar home letter from the Seoul Office of Education, as reported: "되도록 방문하지 말고" ("avoid visiting where possible"). It is aimed at students and says nothing about the figure.
- The mayor and ministers invited attendance ("마음껏 즐겨 달라", "enjoy it to the fullest").
- Newspim (22 Mar) lists the education office's recommendation to students to refrain as one reason turnout was low. That is press analysis.
- **Conclusion:** there is no evidence that 26만 was intended as deterrence.
