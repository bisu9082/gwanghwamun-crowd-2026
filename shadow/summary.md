# Shadow cases for the ceiling trap (compiled 1 Oct 2026, public Korean news via Exa)
See shadow_cases.json for figures, wording, counts, judgement quotes and URLs (12 entries: focal case + 11 shadow cases).
Key result: the simple pattern (expectation-framed ceiling + smaller crowd -> 'over-reaction' verdict) does not hold generally.
Clear shortfalls without number-centred criticism: World Cup cheering vs Ghana (2022-11-28; police 30,000+ expected, 2,500-3,000 present; heavy rain),
Bosingak 2024/25 (100,000 expected, 32,000; national mourning), Busan Fireworks 2023 (1,000,000 expected, 772,800).
Distinguishing features of the BTS case (hypotheses): (a) independent same-night counts made the gap visible; (b) no outside explanation for the shortfall;
(c) the cost of the response was already contested by an organised critic (public servants' union). Jamboree K-pop 2023: mobilisation criticism without a number.
Evidence per case rests on 2-4 retrieved articles; 'not discussed' could miss columns or online commentary.
